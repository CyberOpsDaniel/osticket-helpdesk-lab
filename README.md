osTicket Help Desk Ticket Management Lab Overview

In this lab, I used osTicket to simulate a Tier 1 Help Desk environment by reviewing support tickets, communicating with users, documenting troubleshooting steps, and closing completed incidents.

Step 1 – Review the Ticket Queue

Logged into the osTicket Admin Panel and navigated to the ticket queue to view open support requests. From here, I could monitor ticket status, priorities, and manage incoming incidents.

<img width="1317" height="675" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/817aa2e8-599f-47d0-9647-7e6833d94be3" />

Step 2 – Respond to the User

Opened an active support ticket, reviewed the user's request, and communicated through the ticket thread to gather additional information needed for troubleshooting.

<img width="852" height="520" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bb47053b-1cfc-417e-815a-f8dbc6875e24" />


Step 3 – Document the Resolution

After investigating the issue, I completed the ticket resolution by documenting a summary of the problem and the analysis of the troubleshooting performed before closing the ticket.

<img width="2047" height="1331" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2f530dff-ecfa-451b-a9dd-9c377d42884d" />


Step 4 – Verify Ticket Closure

After submitting the resolution, the ticket status changed to Closed, confirming that the issue had been successfully documented and resolved.

Step 5 – Review Administrative Features

Explored the Agents section to view technician accounts, department assignments, and administrative settings used to manage help desk staff.
